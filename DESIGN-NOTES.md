# DeltaLabs visual direction

References reviewed: SpaceX Starship and the supplied [SpaceX Starmind sequence](https://www.spacex.com/spacexsi/starmind#mass-to-orbit), Eight Sleep, Oura, Nothing, and Lumafield. Large product imagery, restrained navigation, short benefit copy, and progressively disclosed detail inform the design. All mask media uses the supplied DeltaLabs model and brand assets.

The homepage routes visitors into product, science, and team pages. Brain/eye-signal sensing and smart wake, integrated sound, sunrise light, and comfort lead the story. Wireless charging supports it. Intended consumer outcomes remain distinct from validated product results.

The opening moves from a close physical light sweep to slow deployment, whole-board views with steady, rigid surface-mount components and staged local hinges, material detail, band closure, and seated metallic reflections. Shots cut between different framings. The wake light stays off through reassembly and then brightens gradually. Foam has fine grey-black variation with low surface relief and no stripes. The cushion shape is retained. Charging places the logo side down and the folded sides upward, with a slow pulsing indicator.

The explorer blends between authored curved and flattened geometry. A selected component returns through its cleared lane before the cover reseats and curvature is restored. The next selection then flattens, clears the cover, and extracts its component. Bottom buttons replace hotspots. Paired components separate toward their own sides. Text has its own column and stacks below on mobile. Slow automatic rotation, dragging, touch, and keyboard interaction remain available. There are no pause controls; system reduced-motion preferences are respected.

The six simulated traces include variable noise, K-complexes, spindles, eye movements, and brief awakenings. Acquired sample values stay fixed; each vertex uses its original sample timestamp. Colored duration highlights mark K-complexes and spindles, while stage bands and titles travel with each segment. The headline and chart have separate layout regions with edge fades.

Copy follows the supplied YC application: monitor and actively improve sleep, maximize rest and sleep intensity, reduce time falling asleep, and make the product affordable. Tracking aims beyond wristbands without an untested universal superiority claim. Six simulated brainwave, eye-movement, breathing, and movement traces replace the heartbeat-style graphic. Founder biographies describe Harvard, SpaceX, MIT research, and incoming Google experience; CEO/CTO titles follow the deck. Pre-order buttons use the configured campaign link or explain that pre-orders are not yet open.

The sleep guide has 35 expandable explanations across clocks, SCN and cellular rhythms, circadian methods, light photoreception/timing, sleep pressure, stages/inertia, wake light, travel/shift work, seasonal light, health, memory, animal sleep, clinical distinctions, and daily life. The course overview and study/media references remain on the research page. Harvard endorsement is not implied.

Founder photos and credentials come from supplied decks. Kickstarter, social accounts, and public contact details remain configurable in config.js. No invented public links or simulated signup are used.

The canonical PCB has separate root, tip, ear, and overall curvature stages. Disassembly opens the tips before extracting the board; assembly raises roots, seats the board, closes the local folds, then replaces the cover. Ear-wrap close-ups include foam stand-offs outside the core frame. Chip mounts use a shared continuous deformation and local tangent frame; independent tumbling and collision-push motion have been removed. All legacy bend-key values are cleared before deriving the browser geometry. The explorer omits fine trace plating while the native films retain it. Material grain stays in rest-surface coordinates, and the raised wordmark keeps margins above the nose and below the upper edge.
